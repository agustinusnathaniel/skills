Add two skills and fold recurring review lessons into existing ones:

- **background-job-reliability** (new): ack only finished or permanent outcomes and check the client's redelivery limit, bound work per delivery, write deltas through one fenced path, fail closed on missing safety inputs, count every silent path, cancel calls at their deadline, keep flag-off hot paths free, index and page at the job's scale, and prove irreversible backfills on staging first.
- **drafting-messages** (new): write only what the author can defend, edit their draft in place, shape review requests, status posts, cross-team asks, breakage reports, meeting prep, and go/no-go lists, then run a separate checker pass and hand over the full final text last.
- **challenge-every-guard**: practically-never cases get no code and no question, cases that leave real users with wrong data or locked out are gates however rare, enumerate every caller and reader before claiming coverage, find why a guard exists before cutting it, keep every check in an optimization, no placeholders for later phases, and let the owning service validate.
- **test-strategy**: assert at the serialized boundary, extend existing tests first, test every claim in both directions, pin output before swapping an implementation, and eval model-behavior changes with named arms and a stop rule.
- **pull-request-readiness** and **pull-request-review**: re-read the merge box after every push, unresolved threads block merge, hold a PR by drafting it and removing reviewers, set a shared package's placeholder version right before merge, give the production safety answer, keep taste apart from correctness, and answer the reviewer's actual question.
- **github-actions-authoring**: a final aggregator job for required checks, timeouts from run history, concurrency groups that keep the newest run, and speed-ups that keep every check.
- **admin-panel-engineering**: destructive confirmations focus Cancel, operator status copy carries cause and next step, dialogs stay closable while jobs run, and irreversible capabilities feed approvals and notifications.
- **delegating-to-coding-agents**: premise checks and size budgets in briefs, standing rules in one place, pause and drop protocols, a rerun-once rule for flaky CI, and auditing a delegate's replies to reviewers.
- **tool-evaluation-and-adoption**: check version claims against the registry, upgrade a whole package family instead of pinning back, and triage dependency and supply-chain alerts.
