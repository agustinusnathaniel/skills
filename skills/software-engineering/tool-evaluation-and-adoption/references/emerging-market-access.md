# Emerging-Market and Geo-Licensing Access Check

Use only when the tool involves financial transactions, trading, regulated activity, or geographic licensing. Skip otherwise with a one-line reason.

## Checklist

1. Confirm availability in the requester's jurisdiction — check the tool's supported-regions documentation or terms of service, not marketing copy.
2. Confirm usable payment and payout methods for that jurisdiction (cards, bank transfer, local e-wallets, invoicing).
3. Confirm data-residency and compliance posture where regulated data is involved.
4. Record pricing in local terms where the audience is price-sensitive: absolute price, price relative to local alternatives, and whether a free tier covers the use case.

Done when: accessibility is confirmed for the requester's jurisdiction, or flagged with a restriction classification (unavailable, degraded, or workaround-required) citing the source.
